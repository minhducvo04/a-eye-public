Works with

Phone — Open the pairing link or scan the QR; tap Start and remember this phone.

Laptop — Open A-eye in your browser, start a call and enable camera and mic.

Raspberry Pi device — Configure the A-eye server address using docs/device.md, then press its button.

Vision Pro (browser or app) — In Safari open agent.html?device=headset and use your remembered phone; in the native app connect to your A-eye server.
